Talkie Radio privacy policy

Talkie Communications · Last updated: November 23, 2026

Talkie Radio is a push-to-talk radio app from Talkie Communications for teams such as security guards. This page explains what the app does with information. In short: there is no account and no advertising, Talkie Communications does not run a server that stores your messages, and voice, texts and photos are encrypted on your phone before they leave it.

How the app works

You join a group by typing a group name and a group password. Everyone who uses the same name and password is in the same group. Your phone turns the name and password into an encryption key (AES-256) and a random-looking group address. Voice, texts, photos, alerts and check-ins are encrypted on your phone with that key and passed to the other phones in the group through a public message relay (see "Relays" below). The relay only passes the encrypted data on; it cannot read it, and Talkie Communications cannot read it either.

Information the app handles

  • Your name or call sign, which you type in. It is shown to the members of your group.
  • Voice messages and private calls, recorded from the microphone only while you hold the talk button (or press a talk key), during a private call, during an emergency, or during the radio check you start yourself.
  • Texts and photos you choose to send. Photos come from the camera or gallery only when you pick them.
  • Location. If you allow it, the app collects this phone's location and shares it with the members of your group: it shows you on their map (updated about every 15 minutes) and is sent with an emergency alert so they can find you. This happens while the radio is on, including when the app is closed or the screen is off. The app explains this and asks before Android's permission prompt. You can switch it off any time in Settings > "Share my location with my group", or in Android's settings.
  • Online status (online, offline, app version and a random device id the app creates), so your group can see who is on the radio.
  • Delivery receipts: the phones that received a message tell the sender so.

What is stored, and where

  • On your phone only: your name, your groups and their passwords, settings, and History (messages sent and received: voice messages are deleted automatically after 48 hours; texts and photos are kept for up to 90 days or 500 messages, whichever comes first). Uninstalling the app deletes them. Android backup is switched off for this app, so they are not copied to a cloud backup.
  • Not stored by Talkie Communications: Talkie Communications does not operate a server for this app and does not receive or keep your voice, texts, photos, location or contacts.
  • Other phones in your group receive what you send and keep it in their own History on their phone.

Relays

The app passes encrypted messages through public MQTT relays: EMQX (broker.emqx.io) and HiveMQ (broker.hivemq.com). Like any internet service, a relay sees your IP address and when your phone is connected, plus the encrypted data and its random group address. It cannot decrypt the data. These services handle connection data under their own policies: EMQX, HiveMQ. Talkie Communications may replace them with its own relay in a future version; this page will be updated if it does.

Maps

The Map screen loads map pictures from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), or from CARTO when OpenStreetMap is unavailable. These services see your IP address and which map area is shown. Your location and your group members' locations are drawn on your phone; they are not sent to the map services. OpenStreetMap privacy policy, CARTO privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to talk. Only records while you talk, in a call, in an emergency or in the radio check.
  • Location: to share your position with your group, as described above. Optional.
  • Notifications: to tell you about messages, calls and emergencies when the app is not on screen.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to use a Bluetooth headset's microphone and button.
  • Run in the background, start after a restart, and ignore battery optimisation: so the radio keeps receiving while the screen is off, like a two-way radio.
  • Full-screen alerts: to show an emergency or an incoming call over the lock screen.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not read your contacts, call log, messages or files.
  • It does not show ads and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not sell or share personal information with anyone outside your group.

Security

Messages are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key made from the group name and password, and each message carries its send time so an old recorded message cannot be replayed. Anyone who knows the group name and password can join the group, so choose a long password and share it only with your team. Messages marked "private" are encrypted with the same group key: they are only shown to the person you send them to, but another member of the same group with a modified app could read them.

Children

The app is a work tool for teams. It is not directed at children.

Deleting your information

Everything the app keeps is on your phone: delete the app, or remove a group in the app, to delete it. Messages already delivered to other phones stay in their History until they delete them, or until 48 hours pass for voice messages and 90 days for texts and photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown here with a new date.
